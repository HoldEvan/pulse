# Appearance And Monet

1. WHEN the appearance selector opens, Pulse SHALL offer system, dark and light modes.
2. WHILE Miuix is active, Pulse SHALL show an independent Monet colors switch.
3. WHILE Miuix and Monet colors are active, Pulse SHALL show theme color, palette and color specification settings.
4. WHEN a legacy Monet mode is loaded, Pulse SHALL preserve its appearance and enable the independent Monet preference.
5. WHEN skins change, Pulse SHALL retain the stored Monet preference and apply Monet colors only in Miuix.
